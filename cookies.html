Cookies

How this site uses cookies, local storage, and form-related data to support gutter service inquiries.

On this pageOverviewPreference StorageForm DataPerformance SignalsManaging DataContact

Overview

Cookies are small browser files, while local storage is a browser feature that can remember lightweight preferences on your device. This site keeps cookie use limited and focused on making the experience work smoothly.

We do not use cookie settings to sell personal information. Any service request details are handled through the contact form process described in the privacy policy.

Preference Storage

This site uses local storage to remember whether the compact cookie notice has been accepted. It is used only to avoid showing the same notice repeatedly on future visits from the same browser.

If you clear browser data, use a private window, or switch devices, the notice may appear again because the preference is no longer available.

Form Data

Contact form data is sent only when you submit the form. The form is built for email-based gutter requests and may include your name, email, selected service, preferred date, and message.

Typing into the form does not by itself create a service appointment. A request becomes active only after submission and follow-up communication.

Performance Signals

The site or hosting provider may process basic technical signals such as page load information, device type, browser type, and error logs. These signals help troubleshoot broken pages and keep the site available.

Where analytics or performance tools are used, they should be configured to support site improvement rather than collect unnecessary personal details.

Managing Data

You can clear cookies and local storage from your browser settings. Most browsers also let you block some cookies, limit third-party tracking, or review saved site data.

Blocking or clearing data should not prevent you from reading the site, but it may reset preferences such as the accepted cookie notice.
