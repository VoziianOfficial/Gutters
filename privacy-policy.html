Privacy Policy

How inquiry data is collected, used, and protected when you contact Flowline Gutters about gutter services.

On this pageInformation We CollectHow We Use InformationSharing and Service PartnersRetention and SecurityYour ChoicesContact

Information We Collect

We collect the details you submit through the contact form, including name, email address, selected service, preferred date, property notes, and message. If you mention installation, cleaning, repair, gutter guards, downspouts, or drainage concerns, we use that context to understand the request.

Basic technical information may also be processed by the website or hosting provider, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general performance logs. This helps keep the site reliable and secure.

How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to gutter service requests, organize service scope, confirm contact details, and communicate by email about estimates, scheduling, site access, and project questions.

We may also use inquiry details to improve form clarity, reduce duplicate requests, troubleshoot delivery issues, and understand which gutter services visitors are looking for most often.

Sharing and Service Partners

When a request needs follow-up from a service provider, relevant inquiry details may be shared with the provider or team responsible for estimating or performing the work. We share only what is reasonably needed to answer the request.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect site security, or to address misuse of the contact form.

Retention and Security

Inquiry records are kept only as long as needed for communication, service coordination, recordkeeping, and ordinary business purposes. Retention periods may vary depending on whether a request becomes an active job.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website or email process can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending sensitive financial, medical, or identity documents through the general contact form.

Your Choices

You may ask to update, correct, or delete inquiry information where applicable. You can also choose not to provide optional details, though missing information may limit how accurately a gutter service request can be evaluated.

Browser tools may let you block cookies, clear local storage, or limit tracking preferences. Some site features, such as remembering the cookie notice, may reset if local storage is cleared.

Contact

Questions about privacy, data requests, or how your inquiry is handled can be sent to hello@flowline-gutters.example.
